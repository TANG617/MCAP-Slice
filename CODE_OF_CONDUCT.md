# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MCAP Slice contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming,
professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes.
- Protect private recordings, personal data, credentials, and security
  information.
- Keep project spaces focused on improving MCAP Slice and helping its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing sensitive recordings or vulnerability details in public channels.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Actions
should be proportionate, documented privately when practical, and focused on
community safety.

For a non-sensitive concern, contact the maintainers through the repository's
GitHub Issues. For a confidential concern, use the private reporting route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and state that the report concerns community
conduct rather than a software vulnerability.

Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest when handling a report. A
maintainer named in a report must not be the sole decision-maker for that
report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
release discussions, and public spaces where someone is representing the
project.
